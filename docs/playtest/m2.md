# Play-testing M2: a GDO base, an army, a winner

## Starting

- `cargo run` starts a game on `dev_skirmish` as player 1 (top left) against an idle player 2 (bottom right). You
  start with a Deployment Center, an Extraction Facility, two plates mining, and 100 Space Crystals.
- `cargo run -- --player 2` plays the other side. `cargo run -- --editor --scenario dev_skirmish` opens the map in
  the editor: F5 starts a play-test of it and F5 again comes back.

## Controls

- **Camera**: arrow keys, the screen edge, or drag with the middle button.
- **Selecting**: click, or drag a box. Shift adds or removes, Ctrl or a double-click picks every unit of that type on
  screen, Ctrl + 1..0 makes a control group and 1..0 recalls it. Tab cycles the selected types.
- **Commands** sit on the QWER / ASDF / ZXCV grid at the bottom right, so press the key or click the button. Esc or
  Z goes back one step, and a right-click does the usual thing (move, attack, set a rally point).
- **Deployment Center**: Q opens Basic Construction, then Q builds a Generator, W a Barracks, E an Extraction
  Facility. X cancels with a full refund. While it's built, E shows the building, greyed, with a bar that fills up.
  When the building is ready, E lights up: press it to place the building. Q and the building's key place it too.
- **Extraction Facility**: Q builds an Extraction Plate. While it's built, Q shows a bar that fills up. When it's
  ready, Q again places it on a free crystal patch.
- **Placing a building**: the ghost follows the mouse and turns green or red tile by tile. It must go inside the
  shaded build area, on ground you can see right now. R turns it (Shift + R the other way), F flips it left to right
  (Shift + F top to bottom), and the letters show its sides. Click to place, or Esc to go back. While it's ready and
  not placed, X cancels it for a 75 % refund.
- **Barracks**: Q trains a Peacekeeper (50 crystals, 5 s) and X takes the last one off the queue. C then a click, or
  a right-click with the Barracks selected, sets the rally point. Right-click the Barracks itself to clear it.
- **Units**: right-click to move or attack. A then a click attack-moves, S patrols, E holds, X stops, and R raises
  the Peacekeepers' Riot Shield. Hold Shift to queue orders.
- **Debug keys**: F2 switches to the next player, F3 turns the fog off, G shows the grid and P the units' paths.

## Things to try

- **Plates first**: put plates on every patch before anything else, then compare with a Generator-first opening.
  Watch the crystals in the top bar.
- **All-in Barracks**: build a Barracks before any Generator. Power goes red (more used than made), and the
  Barracks and the plates slow down. Add Generators and watch them catch up.
- **Low power**: keep power negative and see how much slower a Peacekeeper or a plate takes.
- **Raid the plates**: kill the enemy's plates. A patch mined out goes with its plate.
- **Defeat and victory**: destroy every enemy structure (plates count). You get VICTORY and the game stops. Use F2
  to see the DEFEAT side.

## Known placeholders

- Shapes and colours stand in for the art, and labels, messages and the cursor are rough.
- There is no minimap and no sound. Supplies show in the top bar, but nothing costs them yet.
- Advanced Construction is empty, and the Supply Tower, Arsenal, Rocketman and the rest are missing.
- Debug commands are on in every game unless you start it with `--no-debug`.

## Design questions answered since M2 (all built in M3)

- Q0017 (design changes 0177 and 0139): a shot from an attacker you can't see is drawn only over ground you see, so it
  comes out of the fog, and the attacker shows for 1.5 s after each shot, long enough to select or attack it. You can
  also target a structure or a patch you only remember in the fog: a right-click there attacks or gathers it, and your
  units walk to where you last saw it. If it was destroyed meanwhile, they stop once they see its place empty.
- Q0031 (0168): a right-click on one of the selected units stops that unit, and the others follow it.
- Q0032 (0169): a unit stuck behind others within 3 grid units of its spot counts as arrived (it was 2).
- Q0033 (0156, 0158 and 0159): a move into a remembered building that turns out to be gone still stops at its near
  edge, and units don't re-plan when a remembered wall turns out to be gone. Neutral objects placed with the map
  (crystal patches, the Supply Delivery Station) are known from the start, so units plan around them before anyone has
  seen them; the other side's starting base isn't.
- Q0034 (0155): only a plate's owner sees how many crystals are left under it. Another player's plate shows its owner
  and HP, and a patch without a plate shows its count to everyone.
- Q0035 (0160 to 0162): Shift changes nothing for a rally point (C then a Shift-click sets it like a plain click).
  Right-clicking one of several selected Barracks clears its own rally point and rallies the others to it. A greyed-out
  button or its key says why it can't go ("Not enough Space Crystals", "Queue is full").
- Q0036 (0163 to 0165): if the last sides fall in the same tick, everyone sees DRAW. A player beaten with no ally left
  in a game of three or more gets a results screen and stops watching. A raised Riot Shield stays up after its owner's
  defeat, on Peacekeepers that are neutral now.
