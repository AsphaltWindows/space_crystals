# Play-testing M4: the Syndicate from the UI

## Starting

- `cargo run -- --factions syndicate,gdo` starts a game on `dev_skirmish` as player 1, the Syndicate (top left),
  against an idle GDO player 2 (bottom right). You start with a Tier 1 Tunnel (labelled T1) whose crystal hatch, the
  cyan one on Side B, faces a crystal field 3 grid units away, a Hideout in its Tunnel Area (drawn as an outline: only
  you see it), one Agent, 100 Space Crystals and 1 Supply. The top bar shows your crystals, your Supplies and your
  Tunnel Space (1 / 11: a Tier 1 area is 48 cells, the Hideout takes 4, and each 4 cells hold one unit).
- `cargo run -- --factions syndicate,syndicate` makes both sides Syndicate, and `--player 2` plays the other side.
  `cargo run -- --help` lists every flag.
- **F2** switches to the next player's side, so one person can play both: train Peacekeepers on the GDO side, send them
  at the Tunnels, and F2 back. Player 2 does nothing unless you play it.
- **Debug keys** (on in every game unless you start it with `--no-debug`): F2 the next player, F3 the fog on and off,
  G the grid, P the units' paths.

## Controls

- **Camera, selecting, control groups, Esc and Z**: as in M2 (`m2.md > Controls`). The command grid is QWER / ASDF /
  ZXCV, and a greyed-out button tells you why when you press it. With the mouse on a button, the InfoPanel names it
  in full with its hotkey ("W: Train Guard").
- **Tunnel**: Q upgrades it to the next tier (Tier 2 costs 2 Supplies plus 2 for each Tier 2 or 3 Tunnel you own;
  Tier 3, 3 plus 3 for each Tier 3; 30 s). W opens the ExpandMenu, E the EjectMenu, and X cancels what the Tunnel is
  doing (an upgrade or an expansion it builds). Upgrade is greyed while the bigger area would overlap another of your
  Tunnels' areas or reach water, a mountain or the map's edge. Selecting a Tunnel shows its area in violet.
- **ExpandMenu**: Q a Hideout (200 crystals), W an Optics Lab (150), E a Chemical Plant (150), C a Laser Turret (100;
  once you own an Optics Lab), each 25 s but the turret's 20. The other six expansions keep their slots greyed out:
  they aren't in the game yet. The ghost must lie inside the Tunnel's area (green or red tile by tile); a click pays and
  starts it. A Tunnel builds one thing at a time, and an expansion takes its cells from your Tunnel Space at once. Z
  goes back.
- **EjectMenu**: one tile per unit type, Q Agent, W Guard, E Grenadier, each with the count in the network. A press
  sends one out of this Tunnel's Side A, by the exit path nearer Side B; the panel stays open, so presses eject more. A
  tile is greyed while none of its type is in the network, or all of them are still within the 2 s after they went in.
  Units with no rally point walk one grid unit out and wait.
- **Hideout**: Q trains an Agent (100 crystals, 15 s), W a Guard (125, 7.5 s; needs an Optics Lab), E a Grenadier (150,
  10 s; needs a Chemical Plant). X takes the last unit off the queue, and C then a click, or a right-click, sets the
  rally point. Without one, new units go into the network; with one, they come out of the Tunnel and go there, and
  Agents rallied onto a patch start gathering it.
- **Agent**: Q moves, X stops, S drops its load off at the nearest Tunnel. A builds a Tunnel: its ghost shows the
  Tier 1 area and, around it, the cells Tier 2 and Tier 3 would add, red where they would block those upgrades (your
  own Tunnels' areas, ground that can't be drilled, the map's edge); R and F turn it. The click pays the Supplies at
  once (one per Tunnel you own, sites included) and sends the Agent, which goes inside: the site appears at 10% HP and
  takes 30 s. D builds a Laser Turret (100 crystals at the order; needs an Optics Lab): upright outside your Tunnel
  Areas, a pop-up inside one. Agents are ungroupable: Tab steps through them.
- **What a right-click does** with Agents selected: a crystal patch gathers crystals, the Supply Delivery Station (on
  the plateau, east) gathers Supplies, a Tunnel takes a load off them or, empty, they walk in single file and go into
  the network; an enemy is attacked with their pistols. Guards and Grenadiers right-clicked on your Tunnel go in too.
- **Guards and Grenadiers**: the combat panel, as other units: Q move, E hold, A attack, S patrol, X stop. A Grenadier's
  D is Attack Ground: click a spot, and it throws grenades there until it gets another order. A grenade flies about
  2 s, so units can step out of its way, and hurts everything in its blast.
- **Sites** (a Tunnel under construction, an expansion, a turret): X cancels. An expansion or a turret refunds 75% of
  its price; a Tunnel site the current top price in Supplies.
- **Laser Turret**: no commands. It fires at any enemy within 5 grid units. A pop-up stays underground, hidden from the
  enemy and harder to hurt, until an enemy comes in range: it rises in half a second, fires, and goes down again 2 s
  after its last target.
- **Debug keys**: F2 the next player, F3 the fog, G the grid, P the paths.

## Things to try

- **The opening**: set the Hideout's rally point on a patch and train Agents. Each mines for 4 s, walks to the crystal
  hatch and drops 50 off, one Agent at a time, while the others wait in a line at Side B. Three Agents saturate a
  Tunnel: the design's table gives 12.5 crystals a second as the ideal, and a real field gives about 10.7 (design
  change 0303: that is the design, not a placeholder).
- **A second Tunnel**: send an Agent toward open ground first (the ghost's area shows only over ground you have
  explored), press A and look at the rings before you click. Tunnel Space rises when it is done, and the builder is in
  the network: eject it from either Tunnel.
- **Supplies**: right-click the Supply Delivery Station with an Agent. Supplies pay for Tunnels and upgrades, and the
  Agent takes them to the orange hatch on Side D.
- **Tech**: build an Optics Lab, and the Hideout's W (Guard) and the Agent's D (turret) light up; a Chemical Plant
  unlocks the Grenadier.
- **Tier 2**: upgrade a Tunnel. Its label turns T2, its HP 800, its area 10 x 10 (more Tunnel Space and room for
  expansions), and vehicles may pass it (the Syndicate has none yet).
- **The network**: right-click a Tunnel with hurt units: inside they heal 1% of their MaxHP a second, and 2 s after
  going in they can come out of any Tunnel. A Tunnel's Tunnel Space caps how many units you have, in the network or
  out.
- **Grenades**: put Grenadiers behind your Guards and Attack Ground where the enemy comes. Watch an enemy walk out of a
  slow grenade, and a blast over a hidden Hideout from both sides (F2): the GDO sees the blast, never the Hideout.
- **Turrets**: build one Laser Turret outside your areas and one inside (the Tunnel's ExpandMenu C, or an Agent's D),
  then F2 and walk Peacekeepers at them. The pop-up shows the GDO nothing until it rises.
- **Losing Tunnels**: F2 to the GDO and destroy a Tunnel. Its expansions turn neutral, visible to everyone, and decay
  (1% of their MaxHP a second); the units in the network survive while another Tunnel stands. Destroy the last Tunnel
  and everything in the network dies with it: the Syndicate is defeated once it has no structure left, and its Agents
  on the map turn neutral.
- **A fight from the fog**: a grenade or a shot from a unit you don't see shows only over the ground you see, so it
  comes out of the fog's edge; the thrower shows for a moment after each throw.

## Known placeholders

- The design's placeholder numbers: the Optics Lab's and the Chemical Plant's stats (the Hideout's, at 150 crystals);
  the upgrade's 30 s (480 frames); the decay of neutral expansions (1% of their MaxHP a second); the Grenadier's cost
  (150 crystals, 10 s) and stats; the Laser Turret's (`factions/syndicate/structures/laser_turret.md > Placeholder
  stats`): 100 crystals, 20 s to build, 300 HP, 24 damage every 1.5 s at 5 grid units, a pop-up rising in 8 frames
  and retracting after 32 frames without a target, taking 8 more; the healing in the network (1% a second) and the
  2 s before a unit can leave it; the underground armor of the expansions (6 / 24) and of a lowered turret (6 / 6);
  the Agent's pistol and the Guard's attack.
- Every proposal the M4 slices built while a question was out ("Placeholder (QD-…)") was filed as Q0077 to Q0089, and
  each is answered now (design changes 0283 to 0303). Q0076, the jam of three Agents at a hatch 1 grid unit from its
  patch, is answered too: design change 0294 keeps the collisions, makes mining 4 s and puts the patches 3 grid units
  from the hatch.
- Two questions are open, each built as its option (a) meanwhile:
  - Q0090, what a player perceives of events they don't see: an unseen shooter's shots in its player's colour; every
    player learns of every defeat (no notice shows yet); a mark shows wherever your tile is visible during its fade.
  - Q0091, the pop-up turret's details: one whose Tunnel dies with a unit on top stays hidden, at 6 / 6, until the spot
    is clear; one that gets a target while retracting finishes retracting, then rises; a Tunnel-built turret needs
    buildable, flat, drillable ground and Tier 1; a Tunnel's turret site takes Tunnel Space from its start, an Agent's
    at its completion.
- Shapes and colours stand in for the art: two-letter labels (AG, GD, GR, HI, OL, CP, LT), the outline of what only
  its owner sees (a Hideout, a lowered pop-up), the area tints, and the button labels.
- The Minimap, the Portrait and the SelectionPanel are empty frames, and an empty panel shows its name.

## Known issues and accepted behaviour (from the M4 QA)

- **Accepted behaviour (design change 0336, answering Q0127): an oversaturated hatch.** With the contact step of
  design change 0323 (slice M4-W3) the crystal hatch no longer locks up. Where more Agents work a hatch than
  saturate it, the Tunnel's income stays near its full rate and the extra Agents wait without a turn; sorting that
  out is the player's job, as with Recruits (0320). Steps 2 and 3 of design change 0324 (a 2-square hatch, a
  make-way rule) are dropped. What the step costs crowds elsewhere is listed below. A moving unit blocked on contact now steps straight sideways by its own width
  (`docs/movement.md > 6.3`), step 1 of design change 0324.
  - **The hatch** (the item below, with the tables): in none of the 30 layouts does the income stop (9 before), and
    every layout takes in 10.7 to 12.5 Space Crystals per second (0.0 to 12.5 before). In 6 of the 30 an Agent still
    goes a minute or more without a load (20 before), and none of the six is a lock-up:
    - **Facing the hatch at 1 grid unit, with 4, 5 and 6 Agents**: two Agents mine and unload from one place each,
      between the patch and the hatch square, and keep the hatch at 10.7 to 12.5 per second. The others stand
      loaded behind them, 40 to 70 su from the hatch and out of its reach, so they aren't in the line (0255) and
      never get a turn: one delivers no load in ten minutes, others 2 to 14.
    - **Six Agents at 1 and 3 grid units to the side, and at 4 facing it**: the six take turns at a hatch that takes
      12.5 per second and no more; each brings 17 to 30 loads, and the longest wait for a turn is 66, 66 and 76
      seconds. Six are twice what saturates a hatch (0294).
  - **The soak** with 4, 5 and 6 Agents on crystals: the first Tunnel never stops (12.2 per second; the longest
    stretch without a delivery is the walk to the first load). Its fourth crystal Agent is back.
  - **Recruits on one patch's face** (`recruit_gathering`'s ignored jam sweep): at 1 tile, 4 to 8 Recruits went 2,000
    to 4,600 ticks without a load and now go 210 to 350; 60 Recruits on a field (`recruits_perf`) bring 34.6 Space
    Crystals per second where they brought 24.6.
  - **What it costs.** A unit that used to shuffle a step along its blocker now leaves by a whole width, so groups
    that converge on an occupied point, queue at a doorway or work a tight face settle later and in other places.
    Fifteen tests held them to the old outcome. Fourteen now assert what 0323 makes of their design point, each
    with a comment that says what changed, and one was a regression that is fixed:
    - **converging groups** (0026, 0027, 0028): a unit stopped at the blob steps a width round it and never stands
      12 ticks in a row, so it has arrived by its stuck clock: 12 units settle in tick 83 where it was 53, still
      without a re-route and without moving once arrived. A line clicked on itself closes up into a column on the
      click. A unit that began 21 su from the click ends 51 su from it. Through the choke one unit stops 165 su
      from the click (160 was the bound);
    - **behind the owner's firing line**: of 8 Guards under an Attack, in 1 of 12 layouts one gives its chase up by
      the Failure rule and hasn't fired when the building falls (all fired until 0323). In a corridor the units
      behind close up by tick 240 (200), and one goes 117 ticks without progress before it gives up (the grace of
      8 ticks at each of its four attempts, where it was touched in every tick);
    - **melee at a building**: the Sabres of one building head for one point by its corner; the second is stopped
      by the first, steps round it on its near side, away from the face, counts as arrived out of reach, and its
      Attack ends. Idle, it takes the building up again and reaches it: ten Sabres all fight by tick 200 with
      automatic targeting, where they all fought by 160 under their orders;
    - **a doorway**: of 40 Guards ejected with nobody ordering them on, 31 come out before one stands idle in the
      doorway with no room for its sidestep; the other nine come out once the crowd is sent away (all 40 came out
      until 0323);
    - **Recruits**: five on one 2-tile face a tile from the Storage bring 5.46 Space Crystals per second (6.10),
      ten on a field 12.6 (12.9), and one of them goes up to 205 and 267 ticks without a load (under 160);
    - **stacked Tunnels**: one Agent of each side changes over to the other Tunnel; the field's intake is what it
      was (24.8 of 25);
    - **fixed** (`orders/build.rs`): a Drone stopped by a blocker while overlapping its site (0117) stepped a width
      off the site and its deploy failed. A worker that begins a contact step while on its site now counts as
      stopped there, and deploys.
  - **The crowds the movement was tuned against** (`docs/movement.md > 6.3`, before and after): 18 v 18
    Peacekeepers 0 of 36 fail (0), done in tick 234 (190); the 192 two-block layouts 2 of 5,856 units fail (7), 1 at
    worst in a layout (1); 25 v 25 Guards 0 fail (1); 18 through 18 idle units of another player 4 fail (12); the

- **(Closed by the item above.) Four Agents on one crystal hatch can jam for good.** In the 10-minute soak (`crates/sim/tests/syndicate_soak.rs`),
  four Agents gathering for one Tunnel locked up beside its hatch at 6:54, once they came from the far patch to the
  north-west: the one that had just delivered couldn't get out past the three coming in, and the income stopped.

    hatch, so that they come to it along the Tunnel's wall; the soak's far patch is only the first such one its

    Agent waits only once it is within reach of the hatch. Until then it is never 'arrived': it keeps moving toward

    Its steering never steps back, and its three re-routes bring back the same straight path, so its leg to the
    patch ends and begins again every second, for ever. The loaded ones are out of reach, the nearest by a space
    unit, and now press on where they used to stand: each for a point the pocketed Agent covers or another loaded
    Agent is in the way of, none of them away from it. All have an order, so none yields. In the worst cases the
    pocketed Agent's shuffling counts as progress, the Agents blocked by it share its progress clock, and they
    never even look for another route: in the soak five loaded Agents stood on one leg each for five minutes.
