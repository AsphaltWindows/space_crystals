# Placeholders

Every number and rule the code marks as a placeholder, for a playtest ([the playtest notes](README.md)): what the
design doesn't give yet, so the implementation chose it. A mark says `Placeholder (` and why: "not in the design",
or the question that asks for it (Q0001 is the art direction, Q0147 the computer players). When something in a game
looks or feels arbitrary, look here first; a finding about one of these is a finding about the placeholder, and
worth reporting with its name.

From the count on, this page is generated from the code's comments: each entry is the mark's sentence (with the one
before it when the mark opens its sentence), the constant or field it documents with its value, the questions it
names, and its file. Entries are under their faction where the file's path names it or one of its types, else under
their crate and system; the computer players' are under their own heading, by layer and faction.

Nothing checks that this list is current, as the tunables move (slices M5-X1 and M5-X2 put them in a ruleset and in
data files). Regenerate it before a playtest:

    UPDATE_DOCS=1 cargo test -p space_crystals --test game playtest_docs::placeholders
