# Play-testing M3: the Colonists from the UI

## Starting

- `cargo run -- --factions colonists,gdo` starts a game on `dev_skirmish` as player 1, the Colonists (top left),
  against an idle GDO player 2 (bottom right). You start with an Egress Node, a Robo Portal, four Drones and 100
  Space Crystals. The top bar shows your crystals and your Beacon Capacity (4 / 10: each Colonists building gives 5,
  a Drone takes 1, a Legionnaire 2).
- `cargo run -- --factions colonists,colonists` makes both sides Colonists, and `--player 2` plays the other side.
  Plain `cargo run` is still GDO against GDO (see `m2.md`). `cargo run -- --help` lists every flag.
- **F2** switches to the next player's side, so one person can play both: train Peacekeepers on the GDO side, send
  them at the Colonists, and F2 back. Player 2 does nothing unless you play it. Each side has a camera of its own: the
  first F2 to a side shows its base, and F2 back returns to where you were looking.
- **Debug keys** (on in every game unless you start it with `--no-debug`): F2 the next player, F3 the fog on and off,
  G the grid, P the units' paths.

## Controls

- **Camera, selecting, control groups, Esc and Z**: as in M2 (`m2.md > Controls`). The command grid is QWER / ASDF /
  ZXCV, and a greyed-out button tells you why when you press it.
- **Gathering**: select Drones and right-click a crystal patch. Each Drone mines a patch on its own (one Drone per
  patch: a Drone that finds its patch taken goes to the next free one nearby, or waits), carries 8 crystals to its
  Egress Node and goes back for more. Gathering Drones pass through each other.
- **What a right-click does** with Drones selected: a patch gathers (also a patch you only remember in the fog), your
  Egress Node drops a carried load off now (an empty Drone just moves there), anything else moves to it, and an enemy
  unit is followed (Drones are unarmed). With Drones and Legionnaires selected, each does its own: the Legionnaires
  attack the enemy that the Drones follow.
- **The cursor** shows what a right-click will do: cyan over a patch (gather), gold over your Egress Node while a
  selected Drone carries a load (return), green to move, red to attack, striped when the selected units would do
  different things.
- **Markers** show the selected units' orders: a green ring where they move to, a red one round what they attack. A
  Drone's Gather puts cyan corner brackets round its patch, a Drop Off a gold ring with four arrowheads round its
  Egress Node, and a dashed violet ring marks the node the selected Drones take their loads to (S moves it).
- **Drone**: Q moves, X stops, A deploys (the ghost of a Beacon follows the mouse; click and the Drone walks there and
  turns into a Beacon in 3 s; Shift places one Drone after another), and S sets the drop-off: then click one of your
  Egress Nodes, and the selected Drones bring their loads there from now on, without stopping what they are doing. Esc
  or a right-click cancels; a click on anything else says "Invalid target".
- **Carrying**: a Drone with a load shows a small cyan shard at the top right of its body. Select it alone, and its
  panel says "Carrying 8 Space Crystals" and where it takes them.
- **Egress Node**: F selects every Drone gathering for it, whether mining, walking or carrying a load back. The button
  shows only while there are some. A new Egress Node warps in from a Beacon.
- **Beacon**: Q opens Warp In (Basic): Q an Egress Node (75 crystals, 20 s), W a Robo Portal (250, 40 s), E a Bio
  Portal (150, 30 s). The building takes the Beacon's place: R turns it, a click starts it. W opens Warp In (Advanced),
  whose buildings aren't in the game yet. X cancels a Warp In or a Drone's deployment while it runs.
- **Robo Portal**: Q trains a Drone (50 crystals, 10 s); W, the Scout, isn't in the game yet. **Bio Portal**: Q trains
  a Sabre Legionnaire (100 crystals, 10 s); W, the Lancer, and E, the psionic unit, aren't in the game yet. X takes
  the last unit off the queue, and C then a click, or a right-click, sets the rally point. A Drone rallied onto a
  patch starts gathering it, and the patch shows a green ring while the portal is selected.
- **Legionnaires**: right-click to move or attack; R converts the active group (Tab picks it) from Sabre to Rifle
  Legionnaires or back. Its units join the other form's group in the selection at the press; one in the middle of a
  shot converts once the shot is over, and R again before they are done turns them all back. On the map they are
  labelled SL and RL.
- **Peacekeepers** (the GDO side, F2): R raises the Riot Shield and R again lowers it. A shielded Peacekeeper is a type
  of its own ("Peacekeeper (Shielded)", a group of its own in the selection, active at the press): it takes half
  damage, walks at half speed and can't attack. An Attack given while the shield goes down waits for it to be down. On
  the map they are labelled PK and PS.
- **Debug keys**: F2 the next player, F3 the fog, G the grid, P the paths.

## Things to try

- **Saturate the field**: train Drones from the Robo Portal with its rally point on a patch, and watch the crystals
  climb in the top bar. Press F on the Egress Node now and then to see how many gather for it.
- **A second node**: deploy a Drone as a Beacon near another field, warp an Egress Node in, and move some Drones over
  with S. Check that a Drone already on its way back takes its load to the new node.
- **Beacon Capacity**: build Drones until the capacity is full. The portal waits ("Waiting for Beacon Capacity") until a
  Beacon or another building adds room.
- **Too many Drones**: about 24 Drones (3 to a patch) saturate the field's eight patches. Train more and watch the
  extra ones: a Drone that finds its patch taken goes to the nearest free one, often round the far side of the field
  (the Egress Node and the Robo Portal cut the inside of the field's arc in two), and finds that one taken too. Of
  several Drones waiting at a patch, the oldest (lowest id) mines next, so a late Drone can wait a long time. In a
  scripted 10-minute game with 30 Drones one waited two minutes at a patch.
- **An army**: warp in a Bio Portal, train Sabre Legionnaires, convert some to Rifles, and attack the GDO base.
- **Convert in a fight**: press R on Legionnaires that are firing. Only the active group converts: its units join the
  other form's group at the press, each converts after its shot, and pressing R again brings them all back.
- **The Riot Shield**: F2 to the GDO, train Peacekeepers, raise their shields under fire and compare how fast they die,
  then lower them to shoot back. A toggle keeps their orders: shielded, an Attack follows its target and an AttackMove
  only moves, and once the shield is down they fight again.
- **A fight from the fog**: from the GDO side, attack a lone Colonists building with a Peacekeeper from 4 grid units
  away (its range: an Egress Node or a Bio Portal sees only 3), then F2 to the Colonists. The attacker shows for 1.5 s
  after each shot, long enough to select it or right-click it with Legionnaires, and its shots are drawn from the
  attacker itself. A shot you see from an attacker that isn't shown to you (one shooting a neutral unit, say) comes
  out of the fog edge.
- **Win, lose, draw**: destroy every structure of the other side (Beacons count, and the GDO's plates). VICTORY on one
  side, DEFEAT on the other (F2), and the defeated side's units turn neutral. Two sides losing their last structure in
  the same tick both see DRAW.

## Known placeholders

- Shapes and colours stand in for the art: the load shard, the cursors, the markers (the gathering ones included:
  cyan brackets, a gold ring with arrowheads, a dashed violet ring), the two-letter labels on the map, and the button
  labels, which stand in for icons: six letters at most ("Drop" for S, "Drones" for F, a building's or unit's tag,
  "EN" or "SL", and "ST" for the Scout, since "SC" is the crystal patch's). With the mouse on a button, the InfoPanel
  names it in full with its hotkey ("Q: Warp In Egress Node").
- The Beacon's Advanced buildings, the Alloy Furnace, Essence Refinery and Flux Reactor, the Scout, the Lancer and the
  psionic unit show greyed out: they aren't in the game yet ("Not available yet").
- Another player's Drone panel shows its owner, HP and Shields only; what it carries shows only on its body.
- The Minimap and the Portrait are empty frames, and an empty panel shows its name.

## Known issues (from the M3 walkthrough, not fixed yet)

- A Legionnaire that converts joins the other form's group at the press, but on the map it keeps its old form's look
  and label until the conversion is done: nothing shows the conversion under way.
- A Move onto a structure puts the green Move marker on the structure's middle, over the structure's label. (A unit's
  label always shows above the markers; a structure's stays under the units that cross it.)

## Design questions from M3 (answered or open, and how each behaves now)

- Q0048 is answered (design changes 0184 and 0185): pressing another command while you target or place one does
  nothing but say "Cancel the current command first", and buildings still warping in or under construction form a
  group of their own in the selection, with Cancel only, until they finish.
- Q0049 is answered by design change 0198 (it replaces 0197), built by slice M3-T: a toggle (Convert, the Riot
  Shield) sets the form each unit works towards, only the ActiveGroup takes the press, and the units change type and
  group at the press, so a second press turns them all back.
- Q0050 is answered by design change 0199, built by slice M3-T: when the last building of the active unfinished group
  (Tab to it in a selection) finishes, the group of its finished type becomes active, and any targeting for the
  unfinished one ends; a completion never changes another active group. When the ActiveGroup's last member dies or
  leaves the selection, the next group in Tab order becomes active.
- Q0051 is answered by design change 0200, built by slice M3-L3: a unit whose Attack or Follow target leaves its sight
  stops where it is (or goes on to its next queued order); idle, it takes the enemy on again if it comes back into
  view. The fog's delay keeps a tile visible for half a second only if the tile was in sight. An attack-moving unit
  walks on along its leg. It takes only enemies it can reach without leaving its route or losing sight of them, and
  always those within weapon range (design changes 0230 and 0235, slice M3-L4).
- Q0052 is answered by design change 0201, which confirms the gathering UI as built:
  - a Drone right-clicked on an enemy unit follows it, and on an enemy structure walks to it and stops nearby;
  - while S (Set Drop-off) waits for its node, the cursor over a node is the gold "return" one;
  - everyone who sees a carrying Drone sees its load shard, but only its owner reads "Carrying 8 Space Crystals" and
    its drop-off in the panel.

  Gather, Drop Off and Set Drop-off each have a marker of their own (slice M3-J3, placeholder looks): cyan corner
  brackets round the patch, a gold ring with arrowheads round the node of a Drop Off, and a dashed violet ring round
  the selected Drones' drop-off, which a Set Drop-off moves (it shows while the Drones are selected, as a rally point
  does: a reading, since the design gives the marker but not when it shows).
- Q0053, the Riot Shield, is answered by design changes 0198 and 0202, built by slice M3-T:
  - while a Peacekeeper lowers its shield (6 ticks), an Attack or a right-click on an enemy is taken and waits for the
    shield to be down (0202); one raising its shield follows instead;
  - Lower pressed while a raise waits for the shot to end drops the raise (0198);
  - the Shielded group becomes active at the press (0198).
- Q0054, AttackMove, is answered by design change 0204, as built (slice M3-L2):
  - a unit that dropped a target (it led too far from the route) leaves it alone for the rest of that leg, unless it
    comes within weapon range; design change 0230 replaces this (see Q0058);
  - a unit whose Attack target is inside its MinRange backs off to the middle of its range band (no unit in the game
    has a MinRange yet), and one that can't back off gives up with "Target too close".
- Q0055, the Colonists' economy: on the design's reference field the Colonists earned 9 to 24 % less than the
  design's table from 0:40 on (24.4 crystals a second at 4:00, against 31.9), and 8 patches saturated at 27 Drones,
  not about 24. Design change 0205 (slice M3-K3), a step toward the answer, makes the Drone reach its top speed sooner
  (about 1.25 s) and stop with less drift: the Colonists now earn 1 to 13 % less than the table from 0:40 on (27.8 at
  4:00), within the ±15 %, and 8 patches saturate at 24 Drones. Design change 0236 answers it (and Q0072): the
  measured economy is the design. The table takes M3-K3's figures, target 1 now reads "the Colonists lead from the
  start to about 1:40", and saturation is about 24 Drones; no game number changes. Slice M3-K4 will pin the oracle
  (`docs/economy_oracle.md`) to them.
- Q0056 is answered by design change 0206, built by slice M3-J3: while an attacker in the fog is revealed to you, its
  shots are drawn whole, from the attacker to the target; other shots from the fog still show only over ground you
  see.
- Q0057 is answered by design change 0223, built by slice M3-G3: an attacker blocked by its own side near its target
  needs no special crowd logic. A melee unit within 3 grid units gives the target up; farther out a unit goes round
  if it can, and one with no way round gives up after about 7 s (the Failure rule) instead of waiting forever.
- Q0058 is answered by design change 0230, which slice M3-L4 is building: an attack-moving unit fights what it can
  reach without leaving its route or losing sight of it. An enemy within weapon range is always fought; one farther
  out only if the way to a firing spot stays within the leash and keeps it in sight; a rejected enemy is judged again
  only when it moves or the known obstacles change. For now a target lost to the fog is left alone for the rest of the
  leg, and taken again only within weapon range.
- Q0072, Q0055 re-measured after design change 0205 (every figure within ±15 %, target 1 failing at 2:20 only, 24
  Drones saturating the field), is answered by design change 0236 (see Q0055).
- Q0073 is answered by design change 0235, which slice M3-L4 is building with 0230: an attack-moving unit that gives
  a chase up by the Failure rule (its way blocked by its own side, Q0057) treats that target as out of reach, so it
  walks on and judges the enemy again only when 0230's triggers fire.
