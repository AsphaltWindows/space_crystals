# Q0140 — A right-click on a full, damaged own building: Enter or Repair?

- **Date**: 2026-11-26T05:14:42-05:00
- **Blocking**: no. M5-N2 kept the right-click as F3 built it, decided by the building's type, so it stays Enter.
- **Docs**: change 0325 (`systems/controls/interface.md`, the Recruit's right-click resolution); `factions/cults/structures/README.md > Choosing Recruits, Release and Eject All > Entering pool buildings`

## Question
Recruits are selected and right-click a damaged own Garrison, Armory or Altar that is full. Is that Enter or Repair?

## Context
- **0325:** "Default should be enter if entering is possible (which is most buildings for cults)". Also: "A right-click on a damaged own building that the selection can enter means **Enter** ... Buildings that can't be entered, and vehicles, resolve to Repair."
- **"Full building":** "a unit arriving when the building is full has its Enter **end** and idles beside the building."
- **As built,** the right-click goes by the building's type. On a full, damaged building it is Enter, which then ends or is refused because the building is full. To repair it, the player presses D.

## Options
- **(a) As built:** Enter. A building of a type the selection can enter counts as enterable even when full.
- **(b) Repair when the building is full** and damaged. This changes every holding building (Armory, Altar, Garrison and the rest), not just the Garrison.
