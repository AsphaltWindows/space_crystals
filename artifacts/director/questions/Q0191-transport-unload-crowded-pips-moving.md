## Question
1. **A crowded unload.** F puts the passengers out around the Transport, on the nearest free spots. When the ground around it is packed (units, buildings, cliffs) and some passengers find no free spot, what happens to them?
2. **Who sees how many are aboard?** A selected Transport shows one filled pip per passenger above its health bar. Should only its owner see the pips, or anyone who can see the Transport?
3. **F on the move.** The Transport is driving, and the player presses F. Does it stop and then unload, or unload as it drives?

## Context
M6-ST1 built the Transport above ground: Enter boards it, F unloads it, and its passengers die with it. The doc says where they come out ("around it"), but not the three cases above. As built:
1. **They stay aboard.** The passengers come out on up to three rings around the Transport's shape, nearest first. The first ring leaves 4 su between them and its edge, and each next ring is one passenger farther out. A passenger with no free spot on any ring stays aboard; the player moves the Transport and presses F again.
2. **Only the owner sees the pips.** An opponent sees a Transport, but not how many are aboard.
3. **It stops first.** F ends its move, and the passengers come out once it is at rest.

## Options
1. (a) They stay aboard (as built). (b) They come out at the nearest free spot, however far. (c) Nobody comes out until there is room for everyone.
2. (a) The owner only (as built). (b) Anyone who sees the Transport, so a scout can count what's inside.
3. (a) It stops, then unloads (as built). (b) They come out as it drives, behind it.
