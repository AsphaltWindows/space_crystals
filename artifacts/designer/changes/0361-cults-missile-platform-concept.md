## What changed and why
User: a long-range, mobile, semi-ballistic missile platform with:
- a long aim, a long missile flight and a long reload;
- a quick recovery (Cooldown), so it can drive away after firing.

Details:
- **Built at the Chop Shop**, consuming **3 Recruits**.
- **Tracked**, with a turret of **minimal turn range** (placeholder 20° total), so it must stop and turn to aim at most targets.
- **Not homing:** DoublyDisjointed, so the missile is dodgeable.
- **Area damage.** It **can't be shot down**.
