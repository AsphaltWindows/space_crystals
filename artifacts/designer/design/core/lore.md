# Lore

## Space Crystals

Space Crystals arrived on Earth via a meteorite impact. After landing, they began gradually self-replicating.

### Properties

Space Crystals have two categories of useful properties:

- **Scientific/Manufacturing**: Valuable for research and industrial applications.
- **Psychoactive**: When groups of sentient creatures consume Space Crystals in proximity to one another, they experience an altered state of consciousness — a sense of euphoria and connectedness with those consuming nearby. Over time, the ego and identity of the heaviest consumer imprints on the others in the group. The stronger the consumption, the stronger the imprinting effect.

Space Crystals are significantly neurotoxic with long-term use.

## Factions

### GDO (Global Defense Organization — working name)

An organization formed by treaty between first-world nations that had the state capacity to clean Space Crystals out of their territories after the impact. Their societies are largely unaffected by the psychoactive properties. Member states do not want crystals on their soil or available to their populations — crystal use could genuinely destabilize their societies. But crystals also offer real research and production opportunities. The GDO's goals are therefore complex and partly at odds with one another.

It is both a military and a bureaucracy, organized in branches:
- **Military branch** — defends Alliance members from external threats.
- **Policing branch** — cracks down on crystal-related crime: smuggling across the border, unsanctioned use, unsanctioned research.
- **Licensing branch** — the bureaucracy that licenses approved private entities to venture beyond the borders and procure crystals.

As with any licensing scheme on a highly desired good, there is corruption, self-dealing and a black market.

**"GDO forces" in the field:** by the time of the story, most ventures beyond the borders are carried out by hired private paramilitary forces operating under GDO license, not by the GDO military itself. Who the player commands depends on the scenario (e.g. a licensed contractor venture, or a GDO branch), but the equipment is largely the same either way: anything operated beyond the borders must be GDO-approved, so equipment is standardized.

**Peacekeepers:** "Peacekeeper" is the official name for a sanctioned external operator. It is partly a misnomer — usually there is no conflict at all, and Peacekeepers simply serve as hired security and bodyguards on licensed deployments.

### Syndicate

**The GDO view:** The Syndicate is a large, powerful criminal network operating on the periphery of the cleaned zones. GDO propaganda claims it acts on ideological, and sometimes commercial, grounds to undermine the effectiveness of GDO protection.

**In reality:** It is "Syndicates" rather than "the Syndicate": a very loose category of criminals who operate independently in small groups, mostly for commercial gain. They smuggle crystals into the cleaned zones, both for illicit consumption and for research entities that cannot obtain a license to operate in the uncleaned zones. Under constant threat from law enforcement, they keep their numbers small, because trust is at a premium.

**Paranoia about imprinting:** Imprinting is a direct threat to trust. An operative can never be sure who else is consuming nearby, especially when working deeper in the uncleaned zones where Cults may be present. A compromised identity is a compromised member.

### Cults

In less developed regions where state capacity did not allow for crystal cleanup, widespread consumption subverted weak existing institutions. Cult leaders emerge by consuming the most crystals, imprinting their ego and identity on followers in the vicinity. However, the neurotoxicity of heavy consumption leads to deteriorating health and untimely deaths among leaders, making Cult society inherently unstable.

### Colonists

Firms from a distant alien civilization. Their world experienced its own crystal impact long ago, and their society has since evolved and stabilized around the use and consumption of Space Crystals. They are arriving on Earth as corporate operations to harvest the planet's crystals for profit.
