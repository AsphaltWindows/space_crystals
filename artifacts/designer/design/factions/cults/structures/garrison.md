# Garrison

The Cults' defensive structure. Infantry enter the Garrison and fire from inside it: Recruits and Acolytes fire **as if they were Militants** (using the Militant's attack — the weapons come from the Armory, which is why the Garrison requires one), while Militants and Gunners fire their own weapons. Without units inside, the Garrison does not attack.

## Faction - TheCults
## Constructed by - Recruit
## TechPrerequisite - player owns at least one Armory
## GarrisonCapacity - 4 units

## Garrisoning
- **Who can enter**: Recruits, Militants, Gunners, and Acolytes. Units enter with the Enter command (right-click own Garrison with eligible infantry selected), up to 4 units total.
- **Weapons**: a Recruit or Acolyte inside attacks with the Militant's AttackAttributes (see Militant); Militants and Gunners use their own AttackAttributes. (Whether an Acolyte can also cast spells from inside: TBD.)
- **Range bonus**: **Militants and Gunners** inside get **+1 grid unit of Range** on their attack. Recruits and Acolytes (firing with the Militant's attack) do not get the bonus — they fire at the Militant's normal Range.
- Units inside are not consumed and still count toward Unit Control.
- Evacuation on Destruction applies: when the Garrison is destroyed, everyone inside comes out alive around its footprint.
- **Protection**: units inside cannot be targeted and take no damage while the Garrison stands.
- Whether the Garrison changes their SightRange: TBD.
