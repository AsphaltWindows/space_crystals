# Airstrip

Tier 3 major building of the Tech/Weaponry branch. A makeshift runway where Recruits are turned into aircraft.

## Faction - TheCults
## Constructed by - Recruit
## TechPrerequisite - player owns at least one Chop Shop

Size, SymmetryType, MaxHP, armor, SightRange, Groupable, and ObjectInterfaceState: TBD. Space Crystals cost and construction time are listed under the Recruit's Constructs list (see Recruit > Building).

## Production
Produces Cults aircraft: a small **light glider** (name TBD; factions/cults/units/light_glider.md), made from Recruits (number per aircraft TBD). Advanced aircraft — a large paratrooper plane and an airship/zeppelin — are also built here but require the player to own a Scrap Hangar. **Production works like the Armory** (user, 2026-11-26; factions/cults/structures/armory.md > Training): Recruits enter an internal pool, orders are serial and queueable and reserve the aircraft's number of Recruits, which are consumed on completion. A finished aircraft **takes off from the Airstrip** to its RallyPoint; Cults aircraft (so far) never land back. Capacity, queue size, sides: TBD.
